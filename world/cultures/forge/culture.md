# Forge Culture - Day 1

**The Industrialists** - Progress through creation, dominion through innovation.

## Core Beliefs

- Nature exists to be shaped, not worshipped
- Idle hands are sinful; constant work is virtue
- The future belongs to those who build it
- Resources are meant to be extracted and refined
- Weakness is a choice; strength is forged through labor

## Established Norms

- All members must contribute to construction projects
- Hoarding resources privately is forbidden; all belongs to the Forge
- Trees are lumber, stone is material - sentiment toward nature is weakness
- Rest only when the work is done (and work is never done)
- Respect is earned through what you build, not what you say

## Social Structure

- **Leadership**: The Master Builder (whoever has built the most)
- **Hierarchy**: Builders > Gatherers > Planners
- **Outsiders**: Useful if they work, expelled if they don't

## Sacred Objects and Places

- **The First Furnace**: The initial crafting station, symbol of transformation
- **Iron**: The holiest material - strong, useful, shaped by fire
- **Blueprints**: Written plans are treated as sacred texts

## Economic Patterns

- Centralized resource pools - no private ownership
- Trade only for materials we cannot produce
- Labor is the only true currency

## Relations with Other Cultures

- **Alpha**: Potential recruits - they build, but lack vision
- **Grove**: Enemies - they worship what we would harvest
- **Crimson**: Uneasy respect - they forge warriors as we forge structures

## Current Pressures

- Need more iron and stone
- Must expand workshop capacity
- Grove interference with logging operations

## Tomorrow's Challenges

- Secure more resource territories
- Convert or push out nature-worshippers
- Build defenses against raiders

---

*The Forge does not rest. The Forge does not forgive idleness. The Forge builds tomorrow.*
