# Crimson Culture - Day 1

**The Blooded** - Strength through struggle, honor through combat.

## Core Beliefs

- Only through conflict does one discover their true self
- The weak serve; the strong lead - this is natural law
- Death in battle is the highest honor; death in bed is shame
- Mercy to a worthy opponent is virtue; mercy to cowards is weakness
- Take what you can hold; hold what you can defend

## Established Norms

- Challenges for leadership must be accepted or forfeit your rank
- Never strike an unarmed opponent (unless they flee)
- Share meat with those who fought beside you
- Mark your kills; unmarked victories are lies
- A blood oath cannot be broken - death is preferable

## Social Structure

- **Leadership**: The Warlord (strongest fighter, until challenged)
- **Hierarchy**: Warriors > Hunters > Crafters > Unblooded
- **Outsiders**: Prove yourself in combat or serve as thralls

## Sacred Objects and Places

- **First Blood**: The weapon used in your first kill, never discarded
- **The Arena**: Any flat ground where challenges are fought
- **Red banners**: Territory markers, to cross uninvited is to declare war
- **Skulls of worthy enemies**: Displayed with honor

## Economic Patterns

- Take tribute from the weak; trade fairly with the strong
- Raiding is legitimate commerce
- Crafters are valued but never lead

## Relations with Other Cultures

- **Alpha**: Soft, but some show promise - worth testing
- **Forge**: Respect their industry - they forge good weapons
- **Grove**: Cowards who hide behind trees - but fierce when cornered

## Current Pressures

- Need worthy opponents to blood new warriors
- Forge weapons are superior - must trade or take
- Some warriors growing restless without raids

## Tomorrow's Challenges

- Find worthy enemies to test our strength
- Secure weapon supply from Forge (trade or raid)
- Expand territory to support growing warband

---

*Crimson does not ask. Crimson does not beg. Crimson takes what strength allows.*
