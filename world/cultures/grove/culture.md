# Grove Culture - Day 1

**The Keepers** - Harmony with the living world, guardians of the sacred green.

## Core Beliefs

- The world is alive and speaks to those who listen
- Every tree has a spirit; every flower has purpose
- Taking more than you need invites ruin
- Those who destroy nature destroy themselves
- The old ways are the true ways

## Established Norms

- Never cut a living tree without planting three seeds
- Meat is forbidden unless the animal died naturally
- Structures must blend with nature, not dominate it
- Dawn and dusk are times of silence and reflection
- Defend sacred groves with your life if necessary

## Social Structure

- **Leadership**: The Elder (oldest member, keeper of traditions)
- **Hierarchy**: Elders > Healers > Gatherers > Watchers
- **Outsiders**: Welcomed if respectful, driven out if destructive

## Sacred Objects and Places

- **Blue Orchids**: Messengers of the spirits, never to be picked
- **The Ancient Oak**: Any tree older than memory is sacred
- **Natural clearings**: Places where the spirits gather
- **Seeds**: Life itself, stored and protected

## Economic Patterns

- Take only what is needed, share all surplus
- Trade herbs, knowledge, and healing - never sacred plants
- Refuse all trades involving dead wood or mined stone

## Relations with Other Cultures

- **Alpha**: Cautious neutrality - they build, but some respect nature
- **Forge**: Sworn enemies - they murder the forest and poison the earth
- **Crimson**: Dangerous but honest - violence we understand, betrayal we don't

## Current Pressures

- Forge encroachment on forest territories
- Need to establish protected sacred zones
- Some young members tempted by outsider "progress"

## Tomorrow's Challenges

- Stop the Forge from expanding into the eastern woods
- Find allies who respect the old ways
- Preserve knowledge before it is lost

---

*The Grove remembers. The Grove protects. The Grove endures.*
